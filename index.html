      04 · Why Temporal

      Building workflows taught me why reliability matters.

      I started building AI automations in n8n in 2025, then moved to code-based workflows with Claude Code in late 2025. Inspecting past executions in n8n showed me where multi-step processes break, and how much of the recovery still falls to a person.

      Illustrative simulation · not a Temporal implementation
      A typical automation workflow

      The same steps as a Temporal workflow

      How Temporal works

      Temporal records workflow events in an event history. After a failure it uses deterministic replay, re-running the workflow code against that history, to reconstruct the workflow's state; steps already recorded as complete are read from the history rather than re-executed. Calls to external systems run as activities with configurable retry policies. An activity can occasionally run more than once, so it needs to be safe to repeat.

      Why a buyer cares

      Less custom recovery logic to write and maintain, less manual intervention when something fails, and more dependable business processes.

      n8n's execution logs show what happened in a run; Temporal's event history lets a workflow continue after a failure. None of my projects implement Temporal's durable execution.

      05 · Commercial conversations

      Understand the problem before presenting the solution.

      Hypothetical discovery · the prototype's problem
      ProblemDifferent lead categories need different qualification: a company buying training and a council assessing funding eligibility aren't the same conversation. Question“How does your team currently decide which leads deserve attention first?” SolutionValidation, classification, scoring and a human-review step, so each lead type's criteria are explicit. ValueMore consistent triage and clearer priorities, subject to real-world validation.
      From the S&P 500 opportunity: two questions I'd now ask earlier

      “Who else needs to sign off before this can move forward?”

      Reveals the real decision-makers while there's still time to bring them into the conversation.

      “Walk me through what happens internally once your team decides to go ahead.”

      Reveals the actual buying process, and where a deal could stall after the champion says yes.
